About me

Built by a cigar lover, for the community

West Michigan

I'm a cigar enthusiast from West Michigan who enjoys discovering great cigars, finding the best deals, and sharing those finds with others. I created this app with a simple idea in mind: make it easier for fellow cigar lovers to find great sales, save money, and never miss a good deal.

Whether you're a seasoned cigar smoker or just getting into the hobby, I believe great finds are even better when they're shared. This app is built by a cigar enthusiast, for the cigar community.
